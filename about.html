Our Story

Every meaningful journey begins with the belief that every individual deserves dignity, opportunity and hope.

Founded on 19 June 2000 by Mr. Bhaskar Annasaheb Munde, a Post Graduate in Master of Social Work with over 27 years of experience, Wadmavli Lok Vikas Manch was established to empower rural and marginalized communities across Maharashtra.

From education and women's empowerment to organic farming, water conservation, disaster relief, skill development and livelihood creation, our work focuses on creating sustainable change rather than temporary assistance.

Today we continue to work across Beed and Pune districts, partnering with communities to build confidence, self-reliance and brighter futures.

"Real change begins when people are empowered with opportunity, guided by compassion and united through community."

Mission, Vision & Objectives

Vision

To ensure deprived sections of rural society participate in development with equal opportunity, self-respect and dignity.

Mission

To ensure equal opportunities for Dalit, backward, tribal and weaker sections through social, educational and economic development, while strengthening participation and self-reliance.

Objectives

  • Promote education and livelihood.
  • Organise women through Self Help Groups.
  • Support organic farming and agriculture.
  • Develop water conservation initiatives.
  • Create employment through skill development.
  • Raise awareness on social issues.
