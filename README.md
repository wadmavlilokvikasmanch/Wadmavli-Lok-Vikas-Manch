# Wadmavli Lok Vikas Manch

### Towards Empowerment

*Established: 19 June 2000*

Wadmavli Lok Vikas Manch (WLVM) is a community-focused organisation working towards empowerment, equal opportunities, education, sustainable livelihoods and development of rural and marginalized communities.

---

## 🌱 About Us

Wadmavli Lok Vikas Manch was established on *19 June 2000* with a commitment to support communities through education, women empowerment, livelihood promotion, agriculture, water conservation and sustainable development.

Our work focuses on creating opportunities that help individuals and communities move towards *self-reliance, self-respect and dignity*.

---

## 🎯 Vision

> Deprived sections of the rural society in the process of development with equal opportunity to all, leading life with self-respect and dignity.

---

## 💙 Mission

> Ensuring equal opportunities to the Dalit, backward class and tribal communities and their effective role in their own development.

We undertake interventions for accelerating socio-economic and educational change and improvement of rural, Dalit, backward, tribal and weaker sections of society.

---

## 🌾 Our Current Programs

### 1. Organic Farming
Training and support for farmers in organic farming, government schemes, bank assistance and manure-making demonstrations.

*Current reach:* 350 farmers across 10 villages of Beed Block, Beed District.

### 2. Women Empowerment & SHGs
Supporting women through Self Help Groups, bank linkage, self-employment and livelihood opportunities, including livestock and dairy-based enterprises.

*Current reach:* 3,250+ SHG members.

### 3. Skill Development & Livelihood
Free livelihood-oriented training for young people aged *18–30 years*.

Current training areas include:
- Retail Management
- Grocery Service Management
- Office Executive

Training centres are located in *Bhosari, Dapodi and Dighi, Pune*.

### 4. Mission Education
Supporting children's education through a pre-primary school in *Kalepadal, Hadapsar, Pune*.

*Age group:* 6–13 years  
*Current reach:* 750 children

### 5. Clean Drinking Water Management
Working to improve access to clean drinking water in villages through borewells, wells and community water storage facilities.

### 6. Motor Driving School
Providing driving training and licence support to people aged *18 years and above*.

*60 students trained*  
*35 trainees gained employment*

Driving schools operate in:
- Beed
- Patoda
- Dharur

### 7. Watershed Development
Working in *7 villages of Beed Block, Beed District*, in the Balaghat Valley region.

Activities include:
- Trenching
- Bunding
- Loose boulder structures
- Soil bunding
- Tree plantation

### 8. Tree Plantation & Climate Action
Tree plantation and environmental awareness activities in Beed District to support climate action and sustainable development.
