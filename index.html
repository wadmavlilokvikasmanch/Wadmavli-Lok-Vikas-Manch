Wadmavli Lok Vikas Manch

Wadmavli Lok Vikas Manch, popularly known as WLVM, was established on 19 June 2000. The organisation works for the development and empowerment of marginalized communities and other communities in need.

Our work focuses on creating opportunities through education, women empowerment, skill development, livelihood promotion, sustainable agriculture, water conservation, environmental action and community development.

WHAT WE DO

Our Current Programs

Our programs address education, livelihoods, women empowerment, agriculture, water and environmental challenges.

🌾

Organic Farming

Training farmers in organic farming, government scheme awareness, bank support and manure-making demonstrations.

👩

Women Empowerment & SHGs

Supporting Self Help Groups through bank linkage, self-employment, livelihoods and livestock-based income opportunities.

💼

Skill Development & Livelihood

Providing skills and livelihood-oriented training to improve employment and self-employment opportunities.

📚

Mission Education

Supporting children aged 6–13 years through pre-primary and educational support in Kalepadal, Hadapsar, Pune.

💧

Clean Drinking Water

Improving access to clean drinking water for households in villages of the Balaghat Valley region of Beed district.

🚗

Motor Driving School

Providing driving training and support for obtaining driving licences to people aged 18 years and above.

🌱

Watershed Development

Working on watershed activities including farm ponds, bunding, loose boulders, soil conservation and tree plantation.

🌳

Tree Plantation & Climate Action

Promoting tree plantation and environmental awareness as part of our climate action work in Beed district.

STEP PROJECT

Skill Training for Young People

Free Training for Ages 18–30

Under the STEP project, WLVM provides completely free skill training for young people aged 18–30.

Current training centres are located in: Bhosri, Dapodi and Dighi, Pune.

Current training areas include:

  • Retail Management
  • Grocery Service Management
  • Office Executive

Partner: Smile Foundation, New Delhi

A Message of Empowerment

“For more than two decades, Wadmavli Lok Vikas Manch has remained committed to working with communities and creating pathways towards dignity, opportunity and self-reliance.
