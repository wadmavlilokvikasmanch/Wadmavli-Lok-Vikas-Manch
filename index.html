WHO WE ARE

Working Towards Empowerment

Creating opportunities and strengthening communities through inclusive and sustainable development.

Wadmavli Lok Vikas Manch

Wadmavli Lok Vikas Manch, popularly known as WLVM, was established on 19 June 2000. The organisation works for the development and empowerment of communities in need.

Our work includes education, women empowerment, skill development, livelihoods, sustainable agriculture, water conservation, environmental action and community development.

We believe development becomes stronger when people have knowledge, skills, resources and opportunities to shape their own future.

OUR WORK

Programs That Create Opportunity

Our initiatives focus on practical support, skills, livelihoods and stronger communities.

📚

Mission Education

Supporting children's education for children aged 6–13 years in Kalepadal, Hadapsar, Pune.

🚗

Motor Driving School

Driving training and licence support for people aged 18 years and above.

💼

Skill Development

Livelihood-oriented training to strengthen employment and self-employment opportunities.

👩

Women SHGs

Supporting women through Self Help Groups, bank linkage, self-employment and livelihoods.

🌾

Organic Farming

Supporting farmers through organic farming, scheme awareness and manure-making demonstrations.

💧

Clean Drinking Water

Working to improve access to clean drinking water for households in villages of Beed district.

🌳

Tree Plantation

Promoting tree plantation and environmental awareness as part of climate action.

🏞️

Watershed Development

Farm ponds, bunding, loose boulders, soil conservation and tree plantation activities.
