About WLVM

Wadmavli Lok Vikas Manch (WLVM) is a social development trust established on 19 June 2000 with a vision to empower marginalized communities through education, livelihood development, skill training and sustainable development.

Our office is located at Yash Niwas, Plot No.25, Samrat Colony, Palwan Chowk, Dhanora Road, Beed, Maharashtra - 431122.

Registered Address: At Post Dahiphal (Wad), Taluka Kaij, District Beed, Maharashtra - 431123.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To create opportunities for underprivileged communities through education, skill development, women empowerment, sustainable agriculture and social development programs.

Our Vision

A society where every individual has equal opportunities, dignity, confidence and resources to build a better future.

Our Major Programs

Organic Farming

Supporting 350 farmers across 10 villages of Beed district through organic farming training, government scheme awareness, bank support and manure preparation demonstrations.

Women Empowerment & SHG

3250 SHG members are supported through bank linkage, self-employment opportunities, livestock support and entrepreneurship development.

Skill Development - STEP Project

Free training for youth aged 18-30 years in Retail Management, Grocery Service Management and Office Executive courses.

Training centres: Bhosri, Dapodi and Dighi, Pune.

Mission Education

Educational support for children aged 6-13 years through pre-primary school activities at Kalepadal, Hadapsar, Pune.

Clean Drinking Water

Providing drinking water facilities through borewells, wells and village water tanks for communities without safe water sources.

Motor Driving School

Driving training centres at Beed, Patoda and Dharur. 60 students trained and 35 received employment opportunities.

Watershed Development

Activities include farm ponds, contour trenches, loose boulder structures, soil conservation, bund construction and plantation.

Climate Change Program

Tree plantation activities and environmental awareness programs for climate protection.
