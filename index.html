WHO WE ARE

About Wadmavli Lok Vikas Manch

For more than two decades, WLVM has worked with communities to create opportunities for empowerment and sustainable development.

Our Organisation

Wadmavli Lok Vikas Manch (WLVM) was established on 19 June 2000. The organisation works towards the development and empowerment of deprived, marginalized and weaker sections of society.

Our work includes women empowerment, children's education, skill development, livelihood promotion, organic farming, water conservation, clean drinking water, disaster relief, driving education and climate action.

Our Approach

We believe communities should play an active role in their own development. Through training, awareness, institutional support, livelihood opportunities and partnerships, WLVM works to strengthen people and community institutions.

Our focus is on creating sustainable opportunities that help individuals and communities move towards self-reliance, self-respect and dignity.

OUR PURPOSE

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

Deprived sections of the rural society in the process of development with equal opportunity to all, leading life with self-respect and dignity.

Our Mission

Ensuring equal opportunities to the Dalit, backward class and tribal communities and their effective role in their own development.

Undertaking interventions for accelerating socio-economic and educational change and improvement of rural, Dalit, backward, tribal and weaker sections of society.

Our Objectives

  • Improve the socio-economic and educational status of Dalit, backward, tribal and weaker sections of society.
  • Organise women through Self Help Groups and support their economic self-reliance.
  • Empower rural women and encourage their effective participation in Panchayati Raj Institutions.
  • Strengthen people's institutions for rural development through community participation.
  • Undertake activities for agricultural development.
  • Organise training, community organisation and awareness programmes on social issues.
  • Study issues affecting common people and collect relevant information.
  • Coordinate and network with government agencies.

OUR FIELD WORK

Programs in Action

🌾 Organic Farming – Beed

The program currently involves 350 farmers across 10 villages of Beed Block, Beed District. Farmers receive organic farming training and awareness about government schemes. WLVM also supports farmers in accessing bank assistance under government schemes and conducts demonstrations on manure making.

👩 Women Empowerment & SHGs

Women are supported through Self Help Groups, bank linkage and self-employment opportunities. Support includes livelihood activities such as livestock-based enterprises and dairy businesses, where women can start milk-related businesses and generate income.

💼 STEP Skill Development – Pune

The STEP project provides completely free training to young people aged 18–30 years. Current training centres are in Bhosari, Dapodi and Dighi, Pune. Current training areas include Retail Management, Grocery Service Management and Office Executive skills.

📚 Mission Education – Kalepadal, Hadapsar, Pune

WLVM supports a pre-primary school with teachers and management staff for children aged 6–13 years. The current Mission Education reach is 750 children.

💧 Clean Drinking Water – Balaghat Valley, Beed

A three-year initiative beginning from 1 August covering three villages in Beed Block, Beed District. The project works to provide clean drinking water through sources such as borewells and wells and provides a water storage tank in the village. The primary beneficiaries are households from backward communities without reliable access to clean water.

🚗 Motor Driving School – Beed, Patoda & Dharur

WLVM provides driving training and licence support to people aged 18 years and above. So far, 60 students have received training and 35 have gained employment through the training.

🌱 Watershed Development – Balaghat Valley

An ongoing project covering 7 villages of Beed Block, Beed District. Activities include trenching, bunding, loose boulder structures, soil bunding and tree plantation.

🌳 Tree Plantation & Climate Action

WLVM undertakes tree plantation activities in Beed District as part of its efforts to increase environmental awareness, support natural resources and address climate change.
