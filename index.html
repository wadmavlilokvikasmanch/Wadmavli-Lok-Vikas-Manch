About WLVM

Wadmavli Lok Vikas Manch (WLVM) is a social development trust established on 19 June 2000 with a vision of empowering marginalized communities through education, livelihood opportunities, sustainable development and social inclusion.

Our office is located at Yash Niwas, Plot No. 25, Samrat Colony, Palwan Chowk, Dhanora Road, Beed, Maharashtra - 431122.

Registered Address: At Post Dahiphal (Wad), Taluka Kaij, District Beed, Maharashtra - 431123.

Our Programs

Organic Farming

Supporting 350 farmers across 10 villages of Beed district through organic farming training, government scheme awareness, bank linkage support, vermicompost/manure preparation demonstrations and sustainable agriculture.

Women Empowerment & SHG

Strengthening women's self-help groups through bank linkage, entrepreneurship support, self-employment opportunities, livestock-based livelihoods and small business development.

Skill Development

Providing free skill training under STEP Project with support from Smile Foundation, New Delhi. Courses include Retail Management, Grocery Service Management and Office Executive training for youth aged 18-30 years.

Training Centres: Bhosri, Dapodi, Dighi, Pune.

Mission Education

Operating a pre-primary education centre at Kalepadal, Hadapsar, Pune. Supporting children aged 6-13 years with teachers and management support.

Clean Drinking Water Management

Providing clean drinking water solutions in three villages of Beed Block, Balaghat Valley region through borewells, wells and village water tanks.

Motor Driving School

Driving training centres in Beed, Patoda and Dharur. Providing training and driving licences for individuals above 18 years.

Watershed Development

Activities include farm ponds, contour trenches, bund construction, tree plantation, loose boulder structures and soil conservation work.

Climate Change & Tree Plantation

Promoting environmental protection through plantation drives and awareness programs addressing climate change.
