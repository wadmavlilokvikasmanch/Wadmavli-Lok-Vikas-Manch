ABOUT WLVM

Building Stronger Communities Since 2000

Established on 19 June 2000, Wadmavli Lok Vikas Manch (WLVM) is a non-profit organisation dedicated to empowering rural and marginalized communities through sustainable development initiatives. Our work focuses on women empowerment, children's education, livelihood generation, organic farming, clean drinking water, watershed development and environmental conservation.

🌍 Vision

To build an empowered, self-reliant and sustainable society where every individual has equal opportunities for growth, education, livelihood and dignity.

🎯 Mission

To improve the quality of life of vulnerable communities through education, skill development, women empowerment, sustainable agriculture, clean drinking water and environmental protection.

OUR PROGRAMS

Creating Sustainable Change

WLVM works across multiple sectors to improve the quality of life of rural communities through sustainable development initiatives.

🌾 Organic Farming

Working with 350 farmers across 10 villages of Beed Block. We provide organic farming training, demonstrations on organic manure preparation, awareness about government schemes, and support farmers with bank linkages.

👩 Women Empowerment & SHGs

Supporting more than 3,250 Self Help Group members through bank linkage, financial literacy, self-employment opportunities, entrepreneurship development and livestock-based income generation.

📚 Mission Education

Operating in Kalepadal, Hadapsar (Pune), WLVM runs a pre-primary education programme in partnership with Smile Foundation, providing quality education for children aged 6–13 years.

💼 STEP Skill Development

Free training for youth aged 18–30 in Retail Management, Grocery Service Management and Office Executive under the STEP Project with Smile Foundation across Bhosari, Dapodi, Dighi and Pune.

🚗 Motor Driving School

Operating in Beed, Patoda and Dharur, WLVM provides professional driving training, driving licences and employment opportunities. Over 60 trainees have completed training and 35 have secured employment.

💧 Clean Drinking Water

Providing sustainable drinking water solutions in three villages through borewells, wells, storage tanks and community water management systems with Azim Premji Foundation.

🌱 Watershed Development

Implementing watershed development across seven villages through Continuous Contour Trenches (CCT), earthen bunds, loose boulder structures, check dams and soil & water conservation activities.

🌳 Tree Plantation

Promoting climate resilience through tree plantation drives across Beed District to increase green cover and environmental sustainability.
