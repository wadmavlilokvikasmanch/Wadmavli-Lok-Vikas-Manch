Who We Are

Wadmavli Lok Vikas Manch

Working towards equal opportunities, dignity and sustainable development for communities.

Building opportunities since 2000

Wadmavli Lok Vikas Manch (WLVM) was established on 19 June 2000 with a commitment to work alongside rural, Dalit, backward, tribal and other disadvantaged communities.

Our work focuses on education, women empowerment, skill development, livelihoods, organic farming, water conservation, clean drinking water, environmental sustainability and community development.

Our Vision
Deprived sections of rural society participating in the process of development with equal opportunity to all, leading life with self-respect and dignity.

What We Do

Our Current Programs

Our programs address education, livelihoods, empowerment, water, environment and sustainable rural development.

🌾 Organic Farming

Training, awareness, government scheme support and sustainable farming practices for farmers.

👩 Women Empowerment & SHGs

Supporting women through SHGs, bank linkages, self-employment and livelihood opportunities.

💼 Skill Development

Free training for young people in employment-oriented skills and livelihood opportunities.

📚 Mission Education

Supporting primary education for children aged 6–13 years in Kalepadal, Hadapsar, Pune.

💧 Clean Drinking Water

Working towards access to clean drinking water for households in underserved villages.

🌱 Watershed Development

Soil and water conservation activities across villages in the Balaghat Valley.
